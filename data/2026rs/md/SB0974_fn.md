Caroline County - Alcoholic Beverages - Inspector 
 
 
This bill establishes that the inspector for the Caroline County Board of License 
Commissioners must be either an individual appointed by the board or the county codes 
administrator. The bill takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None. 
  
Local Effect:  The bill does not materially affect Caroline County finances or operations. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  The county codes administrator for Caroline County serves as the inspector 
for the Caroline County Board of License Commissioners. The board must specify the 
duties of the inspector, including the administration and enforcement of the alcoholic 
beverages laws of the county. 
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years.

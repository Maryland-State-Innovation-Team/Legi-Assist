28 (II) FOR WHICH THE MANUFACTURER OF THE MENSTRUAL

1 (III) THAT IS GENERALLY RECOGNIZED AS SAFE BY THE U. S.
2 FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION UNDER 21 C.F.R. § 170.30.

3 (2) (3) “INGREDIENT” MEANS ANY INTENTIONALLY
4 ADDED SUBSTANCE IN A MENSTRUAL HYGIENE PRODUCT THAT
5 SERVES A TECHNICAL OR FUNCTIONAL PURPOSE IN THE FINISHED PRODUCT,
6 INCLUDING ANY FRAGRANCE OR COLORING.

7 (3) (4) “MENSTRUAL HYGIENE PRODUCT” MEANS A DISPOSABLE
8 OR REUSABLE PRODUCT USED FOR THE PURPOSE OF ABSORBING OR CONTAINING
9 MENSTRUAL BLOOD AND TISSUE MENSTRUATION OR VAGINAL DISCHARGE,
10 INCLUDING SANITARY PADS, TAMPONS, AND MENSTRUAL CUPS.

11 (B) (1) BEGINNING APRIL 1, 2028, A MANUFACTURER
12 OF MENSTRUAL HYGIENE PRODUCTS SHALL INCLUDE A LIST OF ALL INGREDIENTS
13 LISTED IN ORDER OF PREDOMINANCE AND DISPLAYED PROMINENTLY IN
14 CONSPICUOUS TYPE ON EACH CONSUMER–FACING PACKAGE OF MENSTRUAL
15 HYGIENE PRODUCTS SOLD IN THE STATE.

16 (2) A MANUFACTURER MAY LIST A CONFIDENTIAL SUBSTANCE BY ITS
17 COMMON NAME TO PROTECT CONFIDENTIALITY.

18 (C) A VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION IS:

19 (1) AN UNFAIR OR DECEPTIVE TRADE PRACTICE WITHIN THE
20 MEANING OF TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE; AND

21 (2) SUBJECT TO THE ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTY PROVISIONS
22 CONTAINED IN TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE.

23 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect
24 October 1, 2026.

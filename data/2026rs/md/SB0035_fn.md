State Designations and Commemorative Days - State Shark, State Natural 
Sciences Museum, and Purple Lights Night 
 
 
This bill designates the megalodon as the State shark and The Natural History Society of 
Maryland as the State natural sciences museum. The bill also requires the Governor to 
annually proclaim October 1 as Purple Lights Night to honor the individuals in the State 
who are victims or survivors of domestic violence and express support for the elimination 
of domestic violence in the State. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None.  
 
Local Effect:  None. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Since the adoption of the Maryland State Flag in 1904 and the adoption of 
the Black-eyed Susan as the State flower in 1918, the State has adopted many official 
symbols, as detailed in Appendix – State Symbols of Maryland. The Governor is also 
authorized to designate a citizen as the State’s Poet Laureate. 
 
Twenty official commemorative days, seven months, and one week are recognized in  
State law, as shown in Appendix – Commemorative Days and Months in Maryland. 
The Governor also must issue a proclamation each year encouraging citizens and other 
individuals to observe a moment of silence at 3:00 p.m. on Memorial Day to unite in
